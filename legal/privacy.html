Privacy

AETHER CURRENTS is built without cookies, analytics or accounts. It is a set of static files with no server-side code of its own.

WHAT THIS SITE STORES

KeyPurpose
ac.consentRemembers that you've seen and accepted this notice.
ac.modeYour current performance tier — FULL, BALANCED or LIGHT.
ac.modeSourceWhether that tier was detected automatically or chosen by you.
ac.declutterWhich on-screen items you hid.
ac.bgYour background choice — VOID or CAM.
ac.osdWhether the VHS webcam overlay is on or off.
ac.scaleThe musical scale you picked.
ac.rootThe root key you picked.
ac.midiOutName of the last MIDI output you connected to. Written only when you choose a MIDI port.

That's all: nine keys, nothing else. Clearing your browser storage removes all of them.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Camera. Your video feed is processed inside your browser by the on-device hand-tracking model. The tool is built not to transmit or store frames.
  • Audio. Microphone recordings you make are kept in memory for your session only.
  • Recordings. Video and audio recordings are rendered in your browser and saved as a download to your device. The tool has no upload for them.

HOSTING

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any host, GitHub processes technical request data, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver the page. I do not receive those logs.

THIRD-PARTY FETCHES

The hand-tracking library, its WebAssembly runtime and the model file load from jsDelivr and Google's CDN. Like any web request, these show the CDN your IP address and browser. The tool sends no video or gesture data with them.
