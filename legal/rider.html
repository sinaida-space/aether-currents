Rider

Updated 15 October 2026. Every detail here is approximate, a starting point; each venue and each case is subject to discussion.

IN SHORT

  • What. A browser instrument played with bare hands: on-device hand tracking drives granular sound and a live visual layer.
  • Formats. Live performance or installation.
  • Venue provides. All equipment listed below.
  • Setup. About 1 hour for a live performance, about half a day for an installation.
  • Internet. Required: the hand-tracking library loads from a CDN at start.
  • Contact. sinaida.eu/collaborate

CREDIT

Wherever the work is announced or shown, use this line exactly:

AETHER CURRENTS — concept and experience design by Sinaida Krivchenko · music advisor: Kamil Yegelev (TeleFM) — sinaida.eu

LIVE PERFORMANCE

One performer plays the instrument on stage; the visuals go to the screen, the sound to the venue’s PA.

  • Computer with a current Chrome, Edge or another Chromium-based browser.
  • USB webcam facing the performer, at about chest height.
  • Projector or LED screen with an HDMI input.
  • Stereo audio from the computer to the mixing desk.
  • Even front light on the performer’s hands, no strong backlight behind them.
  • Stable internet connection.
  • Optional: a MIDI interface, if the instrument should also drive other gear.

Setup and soundcheck: about 1 hour.

INSTALLATION

Visitors play the instrument themselves, one at a time. The built-in tutorial introduces the gestures.

  • The same computer, webcam, screen and internet as for a live performance.
  • Speakers or headphones.
  • A play zone of at least 2 × 2 m in front of the screen.
  • Even light on visitors’ hands, no strong backlight.
  • An attendant who keeps the queue and reloads the page if something stalls.

Setup: about half a day.

CONTINGENCY

  • A second computer with the instrument open and ready.
  • A phone hotspot in case the venue network fails.
  • The demo video as a fallback for the screen.
  • The instrument picks a quality tier (FULL, BALANCED or LIGHT) for the computer it runs on; a lighter tier can be set by hand.

SUPPORT

Sinaida supports the setup on site or by video call. The form, timing and terms are agreed for each case.
